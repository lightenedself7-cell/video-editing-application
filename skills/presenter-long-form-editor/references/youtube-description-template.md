# YouTube description template (used for the HiringCafe video, 2026-11-15)

Fill it from the finished edit: the transcript gives the chapter times, the director treatment gives the
beats. Put chapter timestamps on the **final cut's** timeline (after any trimming), not the source's.

## Structure, top to bottom

1. **Hook (first 2-3 lines).** This is all that shows before "Show more" and in search. State the result
   the viewer gets and the main keyword. No greeting, no "in this video".
2. **Keyword paragraph (2-4 sentences).** Plain-language summary with the topic, tools and audience
   named naturally. No keyword stuffing.
3. **Chapters.** First line must be `0:00`, at least three chapters, each at least 10 s long, times in
   ascending order, format `m:ss Title` (use `h:mm:ss` past an hour). Titles are short and say what the
   viewer gets, not what the creator does. Cut at topic changes in the transcript, roughly one per
   30-90 s of talking. The HiringCafe video used 16 chapters across 11 minutes.
4. **Links.** Free resource or tool mentioned, the site or product link, the previous or next video.
   One line each, label first, URL second.
5. **"Do this today" list.** 3-5 numbered actions that repeat the video's takeaway. Good for retention and
   for search snippets.
6. **About me.** Two lines: who the creator is and who the channel is for. (Creator fills in.)
7. **Disclosure.** Affiliate links, sponsorship or "not affiliated with X" line when it applies. (Creator
   fills in.)
8. **Hashtags (3-5).** Last line. YouTube shows the first three above the title. Example for the
   HiringCafe video: `#JobSearch #HiringCafe #LinkedIn #JobHunting #CareerAdvice`.

## Skeleton

```
<One-sentence result for the viewer, with the main keyword.>
<Second line: the proof or the twist.>

<Keyword paragraph: what the video shows, which tools, who it is for.>

CHAPTERS
0:00 <Hook title>
0:23 <Topic 2>
...

LINKS
<Label>: <URL>

DO THIS TODAY
1. ...
2. ...
3. ...

ABOUT ME
<creator to fill in>

DISCLOSURE
<creator to fill in, if any>

#Tag1 #Tag2 #Tag3
```

## Rules
- Never invent links, claims or numbers. Leave a clearly marked placeholder for anything the creator
  has not given.
- Use the numbers as spoken in the video.
- Compare against a strong example from the same niche before finalising; add anything the example does
  that the draft lacks (clear hook, chapters, links, call to action).
- Do not include the creator's private details; only what they chose to publish.
