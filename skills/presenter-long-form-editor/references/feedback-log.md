## 2026-11-07: Baked presenter bubble inside framed screen recordings

Artifact: budget-free-website-startup-guide-clean (clean-creator), plan review
Observed issue: plan framed the creator's edge-to-edge screen recordings, which already contain a round presenter bubble. The creator said: "Keep the bubble round, and add a thin raspberry glow around the circle only. Do not add any rectangle or box around the bubble."
Correction: the screen sits in the rounded glowing window. The bubble is tracked per frame and redrawn on top as a circle with its own thin raspberry glow ring, so the window border passes under it and no box surrounds it.
Reusable lesson: when framing screen recordings that already contain a presenter bubble, keep the bubble circular with a glow on the circle only, and never put a rectangular chrome around it. One project so far, so not yet promoted to brand-system.md.

## 2026-11-07: Talking stretches too static; small UI targets not emphasised

Artifact: budget-free-website-startup-guide-clean (clean-creator), first render
Observed issue: the opening, where the creator only talks, had too little motion. Small controls and website addresses in screen recordings were not zoomed. The static vs dynamic section moved the presenter into a side column.
Correction: added behind-the-head and split-word titles, ringed number lists over the presenter, glow-focus roadmap, named-rule highlight, keyword-only copy, UI control and address-bar zooms, a two-sided compare that keeps the presenter full-frame, and range-only revisions with a 640p preview.
Reusable lesson: on talking stretches add a small change every 10-20 s, keep face and room visible, zoom to any control or URL the creator names, and revise only the requested range.

## 2026-11-07: Synthetic sound effects rejected

Artifact: budget-free-website-startup-guide-clean, opening revision
Observed issue: the creator listened to synthetic whooshes, pops and a low thump on graphic cues and did not like them ("a bit sloppy"), even after a second, cleaner version.
Correction: removed all sound effects; the opening keeps the creator's original audio only.
Reusable lesson: no sound effects and no music unless the creator supplies or approves real sound files. Do not synthesise SFX.
